# Pictures for "Write it": Canva AI prompts

Put the finished PNG files in the `img` folder with the exact file names below. The app picks them up by name.
Why the colours are written into every prompt: the app checks the colour words pupils write (for example "the orange cat")
against the colours of the pictures, so each picture must use the colours in its prompt.

## A. Style line (paste at the START of every prompt)
```
Cute flat cartoon illustration for 6-year-old children, thick dark navy outline, bright simple colours, plain white background, no shadows, no text, no letters, no labels, nothing else in the picture.
```

## B. The one picture that is missing (needed now)
File name: `notebook.png`
```
[Style line] One closed blue school notebook with a white label on the cover and a spiral at the side, shown slightly from the front.
```

## C. Scene pictures (20 files, one picture per prompt, optional but nicer)
Without these the app builds a simple scene from the object pictures. Ask for a wide picture (4:3) for each one.
Always say clearly where the first object is (ON, IN or UNDER) and keep the colours.

Colours to keep in every scene:
cat = orange and white | dog = brown and white | spider = black | frog = green | lizard = green with yellow belly | rat = grey with pink ears |
duck = yellow with orange beak | elephant = grey with pink ears | doll = pink dress and brown hair | beach ball = blue, red, yellow and green |
toy plane = red with blue and yellow wings | monster = teal-blue with a yellow tummy and small yellow horns | pencil = yellow with pink eraser |
rubber = pink and blue | ruler = green | notebook = blue | yellow school desk | school bag = teal with a coral pocket | pencil case = teal |
book = blue with a red spine | toy car = red with blue windows and yellow bumper | go-kart = red with yellow seat and blue wheels |
bicycle = green with blue wheels | toy train = red with a blue cab and yellow wheels | handheld game = blue with a green screen | kite = blue, green, yellow and red panels

## D. Check each picture before you save it
- Is the position right (ON, IN or UNDER)?
- Are the colours the ones in the list? (If Canva changes a colour, change it back or tell me, and I will change the colour in the app instead.)
- No text, no extra objects.
